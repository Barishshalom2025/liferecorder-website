How it works

No planning, no pressing record at the right time. It already was.

Life Recorder is a free Android app that keeps a rolling one-hour audio buffer on the phone; one tap saves the last minutes after the moment happened. Audio never leaves the device.

1. Start once

Tap START and Life Recorder listens in the background, keeping a rolling buffer of the recent past. Old audio is continuously discarded — nothing piles up.

2. Life happens

Something worth keeping? Save the last 5, 10 or any custom number of minutes — from the app, the notification, or the home-screen widget.

3. Keep just the moment

Play it with a waveform, trim to the exact seconds, share anywhere — or turn it into text with one tap.

Your privacy comes first

Your recordings are never uploaded to us — we have no servers that receive audio and no way to access it. Transcription runs locally, and only audio you explicitly save is kept. There is no account to create. Nothing goes to the cloud unless you turn on backup to your own Google Drive, or share a recording yourself. (The app does send anonymous usage and crash data through Firebase — never audio. Details in the privacy policy.)

FAQ

How can it record something that already happened?

While listening, Life Recorder keeps a rolling buffer of the last hour of audio. When you tap save, the last minutes of that buffer become a recording. Anything older than an hour is continuously and permanently discarded.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

Never to us — we have no servers that receive audio. The buffer and your saved recordings live on your phone, and transcription runs entirely on-device. Audio leaves your phone only if you share it yourself, or if you switch on the optional backup to your own Google Drive.

Can other apps or hackers access the recordings?

The buffer and your recordings live in the app's private storage, which Android walls off from every other app, and your phone's built-in encryption protects them at rest. Nothing is uploaded — there is no server to hack. Audio leaves your phone only if you share it yourself.

Does it drain the battery?

It's built to sip power. You can also set a recording schedule (only listen during chosen hours) and enable skip-silence so quiet stretches aren't stored at all.

Which languages can it transcribe?

About 100 spoken languages, automatically detected. The one-time speech model download (~380 MB) happens over Wi-Fi; after that transcription works offline. Automatic transcripts may contain mistakes.

Is it legal to record?

Laws differ by country. In some places you must have the consent of everyone being recorded — please make sure you comply with your local laws.
