How it works

No planning, no pressing record at the right time. It already was.

FAQ

How can it record something that already happened?

While listening, Life Recorder keeps a rolling buffer of the last hour of audio. When you tap save, the last minutes of that buffer become a recording. Anything older than an hour is continuously and permanently discarded.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

Never to us — we have no servers that receive audio. The buffer and your saved recordings live on your phone, and transcription runs entirely on-device. Audio leaves your phone only if you share it yourself, or if you switch on the optional backup to your own Google Drive.

Does it drain the battery?

It's built to sip power. You can also set a recording schedule (only listen during chosen hours) and enable skip-silence so quiet stretches aren't stored at all.

Which languages can it transcribe?

About 100 spoken languages, automatically detected. The one-time speech model download (~380 MB) happens over Wi-Fi; after that transcription works offline. Automatic transcripts may contain mistakes.

Is it legal to record?

Laws differ by country. In some places you must have the consent of everyone being recorded — please make sure you comply with your local laws.
