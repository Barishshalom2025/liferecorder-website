An always-on voice recorder for iPhone that only keeps what you save.

Voice Memos starts when you press record, which is always a few seconds too late. Life Recorder is the other kind: it listens in the background on your iPhone, holds the last hour in a self-erasing buffer, and turns the last minutes into a recording when you tap save - after the moment, not before it.

Life Recorder is a free app for iPhone and Android that keeps a rolling one-hour audio buffer on the phone; one tap saves the last minutes after the moment happened. Audio never leaves the device.

Keeps listening with the screen locked

Life Recorder runs as a background audio app, so it keeps listening while you use other apps, lock the iPhone or put it in your pocket. iOS shows the orange microphone dot the whole time, so you always know it is on. Save from the app, from the Home Screen widget or from the Live Activity on the Lock Screen, and start or stop listening from Control Center.

Always on, without recording your whole life

Audio is compressed as it arrives and written into a buffer that holds exactly 60 minutes; every second, the oldest second falls out for good. Tap save and the last 5, 10 or up to 60 minutes become a normal audio file. Everything else was never stored. An optional schedule limits listening to the hours you choose.

The words, not just the sound

Saved a moment? PRO transcribes it on the iPhone in about 100 languages, detected automatically and working offline after a one-time model download. Trim the recording to the sentence that matters, copy the text, share the clip.

The iPhone rules it plays by

Swiping an app away in the app switcher ends it on iOS, so leave Life Recorder there. A phone call takes the microphone; Life Recorder continues after the call. It is not a call recorder and not a cloud service: there is no account and no server that receives audio. Recording laws differ by country, and in some places everyone present must consent - please record responsibly.

Questions

Is Life Recorder free?

Yes. Listening, saving, trimming and sharing are free, with no account. PRO adds on-device transcription, skip silence, the app lock and backup to your own Google Drive: $2.49 a month or $14.99 a year in the US (prices vary by country). Every new install gets its first 24 hours of PRO free.

Can an iPhone app record in the background all the time?

Yes. Life Recorder keeps the microphone open as a background audio app, so it goes on listening while you use other apps or lock the phone. It keeps a rolling buffer of the last 60 minutes and saves a recording only when you tap save.

Why does my iPhone show an orange dot?

iOS shows the orange dot whenever an app is using the microphone. With Life Recorder it means the buffer is listening. Stop listening from the app or from Control Center and the dot goes away.

What stops the recorder on iPhone?

Swiping Life Recorder away in the app switcher ends it - that is an iOS rule for every app - so leave it in the switcher. A phone call takes the microphone; Life Recorder waits and continues after the call. If it was ended, a notification reminds you to start it again.

Does it record phone calls?

No. During a call iOS gives the microphone to the call and Life Recorder pauses. It records the room, not the line.

Is an always-on microphone a privacy risk?

Only the last hour ever exists, on the device, and nothing is uploaded: there are no servers that receive audio. Transcription runs on the phone too. Audio leaves the phone only when you share a recording or turn on backup to your own Google Drive.
