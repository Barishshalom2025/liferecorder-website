Why a retroactive recorder beats a normal one

A normal voice recorder only helps if you pressed the button before the important part. A retroactive recorder listens quietly in the background and remembers the last hour. When something matters - a verbal agreement, a spec change, a phone number - you save the last 5, 10 or up to 60 minutes after the fact. Everything else simply falls out of the buffer, unrecorded and unstored.

From audio to minutes, on your phone

Saved a stand-up or a client call debrief? Life Recorder transcribes it on-device in about 100 languages - the audio never leaves your phone. Trim to the part that matters, share the clip or the text, and you have meeting minutes without a cloud subscription.

Private by design

Nothing uploads. Buffer, recordings and transcripts all live on your device only. That matters in a work context: your meetings are not training someone's model. Please record responsibly - consent laws differ by country.
